📖 Master Strategy Glossary & Terminology

⚡ Quick Warfare & Game Mechanics Reference
🌲 Enchanted Woodland
The central combat zone surrounding the Hall of Might. Castles located outside the Enchanted Woodland CANNOT be attacked or scouted by foreign realm invaders. Defeated invaders inside are banished home.
👑 Garrison Captaincy
The combat mechanic where all troops garrisoned inside a structure inherit the stats, dragon power, gear, and hero buffs of the primary Rally Leader. Garrison fillers only provide troop numbers and tier strength.
🎯 Spire Cannon Barrage
Automatic artillery fired every 10 minutes from occupied Spires. Each hostile Spire deals a direct 1% garrison loss to troops inside the Hall of Might (up to 24% loss/hr from 4 enemy spires).
🏥 T1 Hospital Pre-Fill Trick
The tactic of filling 100% of Medical Tents with cheap T1s before war. Because Sanctuary Capacity = Medical Tent Capacity, all high-tier casualties bypass the hospital and flow safely into Sanctuary for free Holy Light revival.
🌟 Holy Light Revival
Free revival currency collected daily via quests over the 14-day inter-war cycle. Revives 100% of troops stored in the Sanctuary with 0 resources and 0 speed-ups.
📈 Post-War Relief Buff
A +300% Troop Training Speed buff activated after Conquest of Lords that covers up to 120% of your total troop losses, enabling massive net army expansion when promoting healed T1s.
👻 T-3s Ghost Node Recall
Recalling all garrisoned troops from a Spire exactly 3 seconds before an enemy whale rally lands, causing them to strike an empty node and waste their rally, march speeds, and stamina for 0 kill points.
🛡️ Special War Protection
Activated on your home Hall when your realm occupies a foreign Hall or liberates your home Hall. Foreign lords cannot attack or occupy it while active (only domestic lords can).
⚡ Multi-Building Garrison Buff
Activated when an alliance holds a building for 1 continuous hour (requires 2 or more buildings held for at least 20 min each). Grants scaling defense and attack multipliers.
🐴 Mono-Troop Garrison
Reinforcing a structure with 100% of the single troop type matching the Garrison Captain (e.g. Pure Cavalry). Mixing troop types ("rainbow") dilutes hero passives and causes catastrophic losses.
🕊️ Cross-Realm NAP
Non-Aggression Pact negotiated between realm leaders before SvS to restrict fighting exclusively to the Hall/Spires and ban tile-hitting and farm raiding.
💰 Realm Treasury & Colonization
Taxation revenue collected daily from colonized foreign realms into the victor's Treasury, distributed by the Emperor and their appointed Aide.

⏰ Duration: 24 Hours (Sat 10:00 UTC - Sun 10:00 UTC)
📅 Cycle: Every 2 Weeks

🏆 Instant Win Condition Dominion Rule

Continuously occupy the Hall of Might (Center) for 8 consecutive hours without interruption. If achieved, the war ends immediately and your alliance claims total victory.

⏱️ Endurance Win Condition 24-Hour Timer

If no alliance holds the Hall unbroken for 8 hours, victory is awarded to the alliance with the highest cumulative occupation time when the 24-hour match timer expires.

• Immunity Outside Woodland: Lords situated outside the Enchanted Woodland CANNOT be attacked or scouted by Lords from other Realms!
• Castle Banishment: If an invader's castle is defeated within the foreign Enchanted Woodland, it is forcibly banished and teleported back to a random coordinate in their original Realm.

🥊 Asymmetrical Warfare: When Heavily Outmatched

⚔️ Focus: Underdog Tactics, Spire Bleed & Point Denial
🥊
THE UNDERDOG RULE: When facing a mega-whale realm, never fight head-on at the Hall of Might. Shift 100% to Asymmetrical Guerrilla Warfare to bleed their army via artillery, deny them kill points, and maximize your alliance rewards.
🎯 1. The "Spire Bleed" Tactic 24% Loss / Hr

Do not suicide rallies into the whale's Hall of Might. Split into 4 mobile squads to capture and rotate the 4 outer Spires. Controlling 3–4 Spires deals 18% to 24% automatic troop loss per hour directly to the whale's Hall garrison with zero combat casualties on your side!

👻 2. The T-3s Ghost Recall Trick Anti-Rally Exploit

When an enemy whale rallies your Spire, hold it until T-minus 3 seconds before impact, then recall all troops at once. The whale hits an empty building—wasting 5 minutes of rally time and march speeds for 0 kills! Immediately re-occupy with fast cavalry 5s later.

🛡️ 3. 100% Point & Kill Denial Starve the Whales

Whales play SvS for leaderboard kill points. Keep your entire home realm under 24-hour peace shields, and only use pure T1 armies on the battlefield. Killing T1s gives the attacker 0 kill points, starving them of rank points while giving you full 12k milestone loss points.

🕊️ 4. The "Spire-Share" Damage Control NAP Diplomatic Win

If outmatched, negotiate with foreign leadership to concede the Hall/Emperor title uncontested in exchange for guaranteed ownership of 2 Spires and a strict no-raid agreement. Whales get an easy Emperor crown, and your alliance safely gets all 12k points and 2,000 occupation points!

🏹 5. Cross-Realm Farm Hunting (Hit & Run)

While the enemy realm's top spenders are occupied defending the Hall of Might, dispatch 2–3 fast solo raiders to the enemy realm's outer territory. Scan for lazy unshielded farm castles, plunder hundreds of millions in RSS, and port back before their defense squads notice.
